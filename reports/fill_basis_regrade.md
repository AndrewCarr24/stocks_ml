# Fill-basis re-grade of the champion campaign

Generated by `ops/fill_basis_regrade.py` on 2026-09-03. `selection.simulate` now runs the live job's ledger (`stocks_ml.ledger`): a rank date's target weights fill at the next session's open, 5 bp a side on every trade, rebalances under 0.5% of NAV skipped; `slice_row` grades a pick from the first open after its rank date to the first open k weeks later, the training labels' basis. Every campaign number before this was on the close basis (a pick bought at the rank date's close, the book rebalanced free each week, 10 bp on the rotated sleeve). Below, each result on the fill basis beside the close-basis number of record (reports/rank_date_regrade.md). Selection window 2006-01-01 -> 2024-07-18; the holdout (2024-07-19+) is untouched.

## Notes

- The fully mechanical `stocks-ml select --stage cascade` on the fill-basis files freezes 4w / 5y / top-3 / 60/40 / stop -0.25 / cap None (ledger `select_2006-01-01_2024-07-18`; on the close basis it froze 4w / 5y / top-3 / 60-40 / stop -25% / no cap). `run_cascade` decides the book on the sampled 5-year sweep rows thinned by `iloc[::4]` and grades the later layers on that book; the champion's top-6, 70/30, no stop and cap 2 were the owner's documented picks, and on the population holdings the book argmax is top-6 on both bases.
- The sampled horizon check does not resolve the basis effect on 1w: fill - close -9.5%/yr paired, t -0.8 (4w: -1.2, t -0.5), and a sampled 1w level carries an SE of 25%/yr (the population 1w grid's arithmetic net is +17.0%/yr over 963 weeks, +28.0 at the sampled weeks). The horizon decision stands on the close basis, where 4w leads 1w by +3.6%/yr compounded. A close-basis grade credits each pick with the weekend gap after its rank date, which the deployed book never earns, and 1w takes four times as many of those gaps a year as 4w; on the sample the 1w top-6 gap in at entry (+33 bp) exceeds the gap at exit (+17 bp), so the close basis flatters the shorter horizon if anything. A fill-basis horizon layer at population scale needs a full 1w/2y refit (about 1,200 weeks), not run.
- The window sweeps are fresh refits at the campaign's sampled weeks, one run for all five windows; the campaign's own rows came from separate runs (near-tied scores order differently in a few percent of weeks). The holdings keep the champion's cached rankings verbatim, so the book, floor, stop and cap layers and the package differ from the close basis only by the fill rules.
- Not re-graded: the population grid (`pop_*` ledger rows, close basis), the 2y-engine layer rows (`layers_*`, `exit_*`) and the legacy weekly pipeline (tag `legacy-final`).
